Rainel — sliding match puzzle game for iOS and Android

Rainel (Rainbow Elimination) is a colorful sliding puzzle game. Slide a row or column to line up balls — matches are found in the perpendicular direction, which makes it play differently from classic match-3 games.

Common Questions

How do I get more swaps or hints?
Swaps and hints refill over time, or you can buy extra packs from the in-game shop.

I want to remove ads.
Use the "Remove Ads" option in the shop to disable ads permanently.

I lost my progress after reinstalling.
Progress is currently stored on your device. Reinstalling the app or switching devices may reset your level and inventory.

How do I restore a purchase?
Contact support below with your purchase receipt or order ID and we'll help you sort it out.

Need Help?

If you have a question, found a bug, or want to report an issue with ads or purchases, send us an email and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
