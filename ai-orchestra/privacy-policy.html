Privacy Policy

AI Orchestra — Last updated: March 27, 2026

Short version: AI Orchestra does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything stays on your Mac.

What AI Orchestra Does

AI Orchestra is a macOS menu bar app that reads your local Claude configuration file (.claude.json) to display and manage your Claude Code projects. All data processing happens entirely on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

AI Orchestra does not collect any data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, account details)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to any server
  • File paths, project names, or any content from your projects
  • Device identifiers or system information

Local File Access

AI Orchestra requests permission to read your home folder solely to locate your .claude.json configuration file. This access is used only to display your projects within the app. No file contents are transmitted anywhere.

Access is granted through macOS's standard security-scoped bookmarks mechanism and can be revoked at any time via System Settings → Privacy & Security.

No Network Requests

AI Orchestra makes no network requests. It has no servers, no backend, and no cloud components. It operates entirely offline.

Third-Party Services

AI Orchestra does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

AI Orchestra does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Since no data is collected, changes would only reflect new features that might alter that.
